Programs and their prerequisites

What this covers. The Grade 12 university (U) and university/college (M) courses that Ontario universities count toward admission, the college (C) courses that college programs name as prerequisites, and the Grade 11 course each one builds on. Course codes, titles and prerequisite links match the Ministry of Education's Course Descriptions and Prerequisites listing (updated August 2026). Focus courses in the arts and emphasis courses in technology use variant codes and are not listed separately. Workplace, open and other college-level courses are left out. They count toward the diploma but are not named as program prerequisites.

What it does not cover. Prerequisites shown are the typical set across Ontario universities and Ontario public colleges. College programs elsewhere in Canada set requirements against their own province's courses. A university-level course always satisfies a college-level requirement in the same subject, and where a college asks for Grade 11 or 12 biology or chemistry, the Grade 12 U course counts too. Individual schools differ, and some add minimum grades, supplementary applications, portfolios or auditions. Every university program also needs six Grade 12 U/M credits and a competitive average. College prerequisites were checked in October 2026 against program pages at Durham, Georgian, Mohawk, Conestoga and Lambton colleges. The skilled trades and cook and baker entries are the college programs that prepare for those trades. Licensing as an electrician, plumber or refrigeration mechanic comes through an apprenticeship, which has its own entry rules. ontariocolleges.ca does not list course prerequisites itself and sends applicants to each college's page. Confirm on OUInfo or the school's own admissions page before choosing courses. French-language programs accept FRA4U in place of ENG4U.
